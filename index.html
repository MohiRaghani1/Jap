Free Naam Jap Voice Counter

Naam Jap Voice Counter ek free online tool hai jo mantra repetition, naam simran, meditation aur devotional practice ko track karne mein madad karta hai.

Input box mein apna poora naam ya mantra enter karein. Phrase mein kitne bhi words ho sakte hain. Language choose karke microphone start karein aur wahi poora phrase clear voice mein repeat karein.

Example: Agar input mein Shri Radhe Radhe likha hai, toh poora Shri Radhe Radhe bolne par ek count hoga.

Important: Yeh tool personal meditation aur devotional practice ke liye hai. Count ko official medical, legal, financial ya religious record na samjhein.

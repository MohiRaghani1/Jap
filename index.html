About voice recognition

  • 1
    How it works

    Your browser turns spoken words into text, and the counter compares that text with your selected phrase.

  • 2
    Recognition may vary

    Results can vary by browser, device, language and surrounding noise.

  • 3
    For better results

    Use a modern Chrome or Edge browser, select the matching language and speak clearly in a quiet room.

  • 4
    Microphone permission

    Microphone access is required only while listening. You can stop recognition at any time using the Stop button.

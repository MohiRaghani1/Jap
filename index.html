About voice recognition

Your browser uses speech recognition to turn spoken words into text. The counter compares that recognized text with the phrase you entered.

Recognition can vary by browser, device, language and surroundings. For best results, use a modern Chrome or Edge browser, select the matching language and speak clearly in a quiet room.

Microphone access is required only while listening. You can stop recognition at any time using the Stop button.

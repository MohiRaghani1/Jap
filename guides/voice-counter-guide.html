How to Use the Voice Counter

To use this voice counter effectively, first enter the name or mantra you wish to count into the designated input box[cite: 13].

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Type your desired Jap or name into the input box[cite: 13].
  2. Separate multiple names using commas, such as: ram, raam[cite: 13].
  3. Select either Hindi or English as your recognition language according to your speech[cite: 13].
  4. Click the Start Listening button[cite: 13].
  5. Allow microphone permissions when prompted by your browser[cite: 13].
  6. Begin reciting your mantra in a clear voice[cite: 13].
  7. Click the Stop button once your practice session is complete[cite: 13].

How to Reset Your Count

If you wish to restart your count from zero, click the Reset button[cite: 13]. Resetting permanently removes the currently saved session count[cite: 13].

Speech recognition behavior may vary across different browsers and devices[cite: 13]. If you encounter issues, try using an updated version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge[cite: 13].

Mantra Repetition Beginner Guide

Mantra repetition mein ek selected word, naam ya phrase ko regularly repeat kiya jaata hai. Beginners ke liye sabse important cheez ek manageable routine banana hai.

Simple 5-Minute Routine

  1. Phone ko silent mode par rakhein.
  2. Comfortable jagah par baithhein.
  3. Kuch normal breaths lein.
  4. Selected naam ko slow aur clear voice mein repeat karein.
  5. Session ke baad count aur experience note karein.

Common Distractions

Practice ke dauraan thoughts, notifications ya background sounds se attention break ho sakta hai. Frustration ke bajay calmly apna attention wapas naam par laana useful hota hai.

Mantra repetition ko competition na banayein. Quality, comfort aur regularity ko apne routine ke according balance karein.

Daily Routine Example

  • Morning: 5 minutes quiet repetition.
  • Daytime: 2-minute short practice break.
  • Evening: 5 to 10 minutes reflection aur repetition.

Meditation Microphone & Privacy Tips

The voice counter tool requires microphone access exclusively when you press the Start Listening button[cite: 10]. Reviewing browser permissions carefully is an essential privacy practice[cite: 10].

Basic Privacy Tips

  • Avoid granting microphone permissions on shared or public computers[cite: 10].
  • Always press the Stop button immediately after completing your practice session[cite: 10].
  • You can revoke microphone permissions through your browser settings at any time[cite: 10].
  • Do not enter personal or sensitive information into the input box[cite: 10].
  • Session counts, selected languages, and theme preferences may be cached locally in your browser storage[cite: 10].
  • Clear your browser data before sharing your device with others, if necessary[cite: 10].

Future Advertising Notice

If advertisements are introduced in the future, users will be provided with clear advance notice, and our Privacy Policy will be updated with applicable advertising and cookie disclosures[cite: 10].

Tip: Refrain from sharing personal information in front of the microphone or inside the input box[cite: 10].

Meditation Mein Microphone Aur Privacy Tips

Voice counter ko microphone access ki zarurat tab padti hai jab aap Start Listening button press karte hain. Browser permission ko carefully review karna achhi privacy practice hai.

Basic Privacy Tips

  • Shared ya public computer par microphone permission na dein.
  • Practice complete hone ke baad Stop button press karein.
  • Browser settings se microphone permission revoke kar sakte hain.
  • Input box mein personal ya sensitive information na likhein.
  • Local storage mein count, language aur theme preference save ho sakte hain.
  • Device kisi aur ko dene se pehle browser data clear karein, agar zarurat ho.

Future Advertising Notice

Abhi is website par Google AdSense ya koi third-party advertising service active nahi hai.

Future mein agar ads add kiye jaate hain, toh users ko ads enable karne se pehle clear notice diya jayega. Privacy Policy ko applicable advertising aur cookie information ke saath update kiya jayega.

Tip: Personal information ko microphone ke saamne ya input box mein share na karein.

Simple process

How manual counting works

Use the counter at your own pace with a simple and clear three-step practice.

1

Get comfortable

Sit peacefully, hold your mala if you use one, and keep the counter open.

2

Repeat your Naam

Say or remember your chosen Naam, then tap “Add 1 Jap” once after each repetition.

3

Correct mistakes easily

If you tap by mistake, use “Undo last” to remove the previous manual count.

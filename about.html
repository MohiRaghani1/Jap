About Us

Naam Jap Voice Counter is a browser-based online utility designed to assist users in tracking mantra repetition and Naam Simran practice[cite: 4].

The primary purpose of this website is to provide a seamless, hands-free counting experience. Users can enter their preferred name or mantra and use microphone voice recognition to automatically count matching repetitions[cite: 4].

Our core goal is to make meditation, prayer, and devotional practice simple and accessible for everyone[cite: 4].

Please note that this website is a digital counting aid and is not intended to replace traditional prayer beads (mala), spiritual guidance, professional advice, or personal religious practices[cite: 4].

Platform features and functionality operate according to standard browser support and speech recognition technology capabilities[cite: 4].

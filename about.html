About Us

Naam Jap Voice Counter ek browser-based online utility hai jo users ko mantra repetition aur naam simran track karne mein help karti hai.

Is website ka purpose ek simple hands-free counting experience provide karna hai. Users apna preferred naam ya mantra enter karke microphone ke through matching words count kar sakte hain.

Hamara goal meditation, prayer aur devotional practice ko simple aur accessible banana hai.

Yeh website traditional mala, spiritual teacher, medical advice, ya personal religious practice ka replacement nahi hai. Yeh sirf ek digital counting aid hai.

Website ke features browser support aur speech recognition technology ke according kaam karte hain.

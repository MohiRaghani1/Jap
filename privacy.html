Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Naam Jap Voice Counter respects your privacy. Yeh website browser-based speech recognition aur meditation counting tool provide karti hai.

Microphone Permission

Microphone permission sirf tab request ki ja sakti hai jab aap Start Listening button press karte hain. Aap permission deny ya browser settings se revoke kar sakte hain.

Speech Recognition

Speech recognition browser-supported technology ke through work karta hai. Browser, device aur settings ke according speech processing local ya browser-supported service ke through ho sakti hai.

Hum aapki microphone audio ko intentionally record, sell, store, ya apne server par permanently save nahi karte.

Local Storage

Website browser local storage ka use jap count, selected language aur theme preference save karne ke liye kar sakti hai.

Local storage mein save information aapke browser mein rehti hai aur hum ise intentionally apne server par send nahi karte.

Advertising Status

Abhi is website par Google AdSense, display advertising, advertising cookies ya koi third-party advertising service active nahi hai.

Future mein agar advertising service add ki jaati hai, toh users ko ads enable karne se pehle clear notice diya jayega. Privacy Policy ko advertising cookies, third-party vendors, data usage aur available user choices ki information ke saath update kiya jayega.

Jahan applicable law ke according consent required hoga, wahan appropriate consent process use kiya jayega.

Third-Party Services

Website future mein hosting, analytics ya browser speech recognition services ka use kar sakti hai. Third-party services apni respective privacy policies ke according information process kar sakti hain.

Children's Privacy

Yeh website children se knowingly personal information collect karne ke liye design nahi ki gayi hai.
